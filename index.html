Glass Court Squash

A full-size court, one opponent, first to 11 points by two clear. Every rally scores. Whoever wins a point serves next.

Opponent

Controls

Move
WASD or arrow keys. In first person you walk the way you are looking. Touch: drag on the left half.
Look and aim
First person: move the mouse to look (the first click captures it, Esc releases it) and aim with the crosshair. R recentres the view. V switches to third person, where the mouse cursor aims. Look at a side wall to boast off it. Turn round and look at the back wall to boast off that: it works best from the back half of the court. Touch: drag on the right half.
Jump
Space (touch: Jump button). You can hit the ball higher in the air.
Shot power
Hold the left mouse button (touch: hold Hit) to charge, then release to hit. A quick tap is a gentle shot, about one second of holding is full power. Harder shots are less accurate, and aim high for a lob.

Rules

Front wall
Hit it above the tin (the grey board) and below the top red line.
Return
Reach the ball before it bounces twice. Side and back walls are fine.
Serve
Stand in a service box (yellow). Hit the front wall above the middle red line so the ball lands in the opposite back quarter (blue). The ring shows where a serve at your current charge would land (green is legal). A fault gives you a second serve. If you win the point you serve from the other box.
